DUSK - A GUIDE FOR THE CLASSIC CLIENT
=====================================

You're playing with the classic Java client. Most things are done by
typing a command in the box at the bottom and pressing Enter. (The browser
client at https://duskserver.tailaee0f2.ts.net/play/ has buttons for all
of this, if you ever want to try it.)

Type  help  in the game for the original list of classic commands.


TALKING
-------
'hello            say something to the people near you
;hello            gossip: heard by everyone in the game
/Tom hi           tell one player something privately
.waves            an action ("Flan waves")
who               who's online


TALKING TO PEOPLE IN THE WORLD
------------------------------
talk              talk to the person next to you (stand beside them)
talk elder        talk to the one with "elder" in their name, if several
                  are near
(If they're a step too far, you'll be told who to get closer to.)

When they ask you something, the answers are at the end of what they say,
in brackets, split by /. Type one of them (capitals and punctuation don't
matter):

  Taskmistress Vhal says: ... Will you be trained? (train me/i am ready)
  > train me

Handing things over: people in the world don't take things with "give"
(that's for other players). When someone asks you to bring them
something, talk to them while you carry it and type the answer that
offers it, e.g.

  Blademistress Zeerith says: Well? ... bring it to me. (here it is)
  > here it is

and they take it from your pack themselves.

Some people also call out to you as you walk past.


MOVING AND FIGHTING
-------------------
n  s  e  w        walk one square (or click the map)
a rat             attack ("a rat 1" for the second rat)
look rat          how strong it looks
flee              run from a fight
sleep / wake      rest to heal faster (you can't walk while asleep)

The lists on the right (Attack, Action, Look, Get, Drop) do the same with
a click.


THINGS, SHOPS AND MONEY
-----------------------
get <item>        pick something up         drop <item>   put it down
wear <item>       wear or wield it          unwear <item> take it off
use <item>        eat, drink, read...
give Tom gp 100   give gold (or an item) to another player

Stand on a shopkeeper's square and press the Merchant button to buy and
sell, or type it:  buy water   buy 5 arrows   sell apple
Shops buy things back for half what they cost.

Storage: barrels and chests in town are for everyone, bank tellers keep a
deposit box just for you. Stand next to one:
  storage                 what's in it
  storage put <item>      put something in
  storage take <item>     take it out
  box                     your deposit box, by a bank teller


MINING
------
Buy a mining pick from a smith (Brann in Kingshaven, the Izaru Mine Shop,
or the stalls by the forges). Find an ore vein: a mound of rock against the
hills, with lumps of ore in it. Stand next to it and type:

  mine              start digging; you keep going until the vein is
                    worked out or you move
  mine stop         stop

Bronze veins are near the ferry landings. Iron, coal and mithril lie
further out and need a higher Mining skill. A worked-out vein fills again
after a few minutes. Mining rises as you dig.


SMITHING
--------
Forges and anvils stand in Kingshaven (Brann's shop), Izaru's mine hall,
and Ssarakh.

  smith             (by a forge or anvil) what you can make there, what it
                    needs, and how many you could make
  smith bronze bar  make one
  smith bronze bar 5     make five

At a forge you smelt ore into bars. At an anvil, with a smithing hammer in
your pack, you make bars into weapons and armour. Mithril armour and swords
can only be smithed. Smithing rises as you work.


HERBS AND ALCHEMY
-----------------
Buy a herb sickle (alchemists and the stalls by the forges sell them).
Herbs grow in patches at the edges of woods. Stand next to one and type:

  gather            start picking; you keep going until the patch is
                    picked bare or you move
  gather stop       stop

Mushrooms and mandrake grow near the ferry landings; lavender, wolfsbane
and azeala further out; rare moonpetal far away. Herbalism rises as you
pick and lets you gather the rarer herbs.

Alchemy tables stand in Kingshaven (Alchemist Pell), Aelindor (Miriel),
Ssarakh, and by Herbalist Wenna:

  brew              (by an alchemy table) what you can brew there
  brew brewed red potion      brew one   (add a number for more)

Brewed potions work like bought ones but merchants won't buy them. The
moonpetal elixir (1000 health and mana) can only be brewed.


WOODCUTTING AND FLETCHING
-------------------------
Buy a woodcutter's axe (smiths, bowyers and the stalls sell them). Every
tree can be chopped. Stand next to one and type:

  chop              start chopping; you keep going until the tree has no
                    good wood left or you move
  chop stop         stop

Oak grows near the ferry landings; ash and yew further out; elderwood far
away. Woodcutting rises as you chop.

Workbenches stand in Kingshaven (Brann's shop), Izaru's mine hall,
Ssarakh, and Caelum's bow shop in Aelindor. With a fletching knife:

  fletch            (by a workbench) what you can make there
  fletch cheap bow  make one   (add a number for more)

Elderwood bows and staves can only be made.


PLAYING WITH OTHERS
-------------------
party invite Tom  ask Tom to join your party (kills for quests count for
                  everyone in the party who's nearby)
party accept      join the party you were asked to
party             who's in your party
party leave       leave it
party say hi      talk to your party
duel Tom          (both standing in the Duelling Ring outside Kingshaven)
                  challenge Tom; nobody loses anything
duel accept       accept a challenge
duel              your duel record
follow Tom        walk behind Tom (leave to stop)


OTHER THINGS TO KNOW
--------------------
boss              is a world boss about, or when the next one is due
Bounty boards in towns have jobs: stand by a notice and talk to it, e.g.
  talk black dog
to take the job; talk to it again when it's done to be paid.
The website has the forum, who's online and the leaderboard:
  https://duskserver.tailaee0f2.ts.net/web/index.html
