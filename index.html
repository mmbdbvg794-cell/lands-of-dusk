                      How to play

                      Moving

                      ↑↓←→ or WASD walk; hold to keep walking. On a phone, use the arrows on the screen.

                      Click a square to walk there. Click someone or something for what you can do: talk, attack, look, pick up, shop, trade.

                      M or the map button opens the world map. Click the small map to zoom it.

                      Talking

                      Enter to type. Start the line with:

                      ' hellosay it to the people near you
                      ; hellogossip: everyone online hears it
                      /name hellotell one player, wherever they are (or click them: Whisper)
                      : helloyour clan, if you're in one
                      emote wavesan action: "flan waves"

                      Anything else you type is a command. ↑↓ in the box bring back what you typed before.

                      When someone asks you something, press an answer, or type it.

                      Fighting and spells

                      Click a creature and choose Attack (you have to stand next to it). The card at the top shows who you're fighting.

                      1–9, 0 use the hotbar slots. Click an empty slot to put a spell or any command in it; right-click or long-press to change it. The Spellbook lists your spells.

                      Trainers teach spells and skills, paid for in experience. Sleep (the button beside hunger and thirst) heals faster, but you can't walk until you wake. In a fight, Flee is on the target card.

                      Things and money

                      The Backpack holds what you carry: use, wear or drop it there. Equipped shows what you wear.

                      Stand at a shop's counter and the Shop tab opens. Shops buy things back for half what they cost.

                      Eat and drink now and then: shops sell food and water, and you'll be told when you're hungry or thirsty.

                      Bank tellers keep a deposit box for you; barrels and chests in town are for everyone. Click a player and choose Trade to swap with them.

                      Quests

                      Talk to people: many have work for you. The Journal keeps track of what you've taken on.

                      Settings

                      The gear button: night, weather, lighting, music, names, the look of the game, and Report a bug.

                      Report a bug

                      While you're in the game, it goes straight to the gods as a message (they get it when they next log in if they're away). It also opens your email app with a message to duskadmins@gmail.com, ready to send.
