# The Lands of Dusk: the story so far

The canon behind everything players find in the game. Players never read
this whole: each place tells its own piece, from its own side (see "Where
the pieces are" below). Keep new quests, NPCs and items consistent with it,
and add to it here first.

## Read this first (for anyone writing more lore)

The Lands of Dusk is a small online RPG: a top-down tile world on the old
DuskRPG engine, played in a browser. **flan** owns the game and decides
canon. This file is complete on its own: it is everything decided so far.
Last brought up to date 2026-10-29.

**What is fixed and what isn't.** Anything marked BUILT or IN GAME is live:
players have read it, and changing it means rewriting quests. Anything
marked "lore only" or listed under open threads can still move. Decisions
are lettered A to R; a new idea is a *proposal* for decision S onward,
saying what players see and what stays secret, until flan agrees.

**House rules for writing.** These hold in the live game; they aren't
suggestions.

1. **Short NPC lines.** A one-line greeting, then topics the player picks
   ("This place?", "The desert?"), each answered in a sentence or two. Never
   one long speech.
2. **Keep the mysteries.** Hidden places get rumours at most: no signposts,
   no maps, no directions. Nobody knows everything; each people tells its
   own side, and some of it is wrong (the surface elves on the Sundering,
   Faelith on the wind).
3. **Signs show names only:** "Last Water", "The Silver Leaf". A warning
   ("Turn back.") or an old inscription may say more. Prices, directions and
   how-to belong in NPC talk. Tutorials are the only exception.
4. **Nothing out of the world:** no modern words and no builders' jokes
   (decision H).
5. **The old world is emptying, not burning** (decision D).
6. **Check names** against the list under "Where the pieces are" before
   adding a person (decision E fixed old clashes).
7. **Secrets stay secret.** Decision C, and the crown's power in K, are
   never said in the game. Hints are allowed.

**What a quest can be.** Quests are made in the game's quest wizard, as
steps of four kinds: *kill* N of a monster, *go to* a place, *talk to* an
NPC, and *bring* an item. Each sets flags. Rewards are gold, experience and
items, and sometimes an achievement. NPCs can appear or vanish by flag, and
their lines can change after a quest. Every story is told in each player's
own world: one player's rift is closed while another's is still open.
Write new stories in that shape.

**Who players are.** The races are human, elf, dark elf (drow) and
lizardfolk. Each race begins in its own tutorial:

| Race | Tutorial | Where |
|---|---|---|
| Human | *From Refugee to Soldier...* | Westsea, in the old world (decision I) |
| Elf | *From Seedling to Sentinel...* | the Grove of Waking, by Aelindor |
| Dark elf | *From Hatchling to Drow...* | Velkarath |
| Lizardfolk | *From Hatchling to Brood-Guard...* | the Hatching Pools, on the east isle |

The classes are Fighter, Mage, Cleric, Ranger, Paladin, and Priest or
Priestess (drow only). Three schools teach their members only:

- **the Mage Guild**, in a tower on the isle off Eastreach (no ferry goes
  there yet);
- **The Circle**, nature lore, in a grove deep in the wood east of
  Aelindor;
- **the Drow Priesthood**, in the Temple of the Spider Queen in Velkarath.

None of the schools has its invitation story yet.

## The fall of Zilen (the old world)

- The old world is the kingdom of **Zilen**: the City of Light (its
  capital), Midnight and the Izaru mines, Riverside, Westsea Harbour, and
  Firetown, a forge town on an island in the southern sea.
- **King Zilen** was a good king. He lies in the City of Light's graveyard
  beside his queen, kept by Tombkeeper Aldous. His elder son **Aldric** took
  the throne; the younger, **Morvane**, a sorcerer, was passed over and
  never forgave it.
- Under Firetown lies one of the **seven gates**: doors under the world to
  places that should never touch it. In the first age they were sealed with
  **warden stones** cut by the lizardfolk elders. The Drow went below to
  guard them.
- Forty years ago Morvane came to Velkarath's markets asking about the gate
  under Firetown. The Drow sold him nothing; he found it anyway. He wanted
  an army that could not die, and he **opened the gate**.
- Through it came the **Ashen Horde**, out of a burning realm, led by
  **Kraghul**, a horned and scaled warlord. Its price for Morvane was paid
  in the dead: everyone the Horde killed rose for him. In the end Morvane
  died too and rose as the **Skeleton King**. Kraghul burns; Morvane raises
  what burns.
- Firetown fell first; the rift stood open there forty years, and no one
  went, until Old Bram's boat (see The Firetown Expedition). Then the
  Horde came west. Midnight's deep mines were overrun (the Izaru Deeps),
  King Zilen's grave was robbed of its crown and the crypt of his guard
  defiled (the Zilen Legacy). **The City of Light still holds**, barely.
  The old world is a dying land, dying by emptying, not burning: most of
  its people sailed east, and the ones left are stubborn, poor or old.
  Adventurers go back to fight in it.
- King Aldric proclaimed Morvane a traitor, closed the road to Firetown, and
  sent his people away by sea. He did not come east himself, and he is gone
  now: dead or missing, no one knows (see "Canon decisions").

## The crossing

- Forty ships sailed **east** from Westsea Harbour, packed with refugees.
  Storms took nine. The rest reached the empty coast of the new lands and
  built **Kingshaven** out of their own ships.
- Aldric sent his daughter, **Princess Elowen**, on the last ship, the
  **Dawnward**, to rule the refugees. The Dawnward never arrived. Kingshaven
  ("the king's haven") keeps an empty throne for her. **Lord Steward
  Halvard** governs the city in her name, standing beside the empty chair.
- **What became of her** (the quest chain *The Dawnward*, dawnward.mjs): a
  storm wrecked the Dawnward on the lizardman isles' east shore (the wreck
  is still there, stripped by Dry One raiders). The brood pulled Elowen,
  then about sixteen, from the water. She lived a year in Ssarakh, learned
  from Shaman Ithrae how the gates were sealed, and left with the brood's
  blessing and a shard of the Brood Stone. Twelve years ago, grey-haired,
  she knelt at King Zilen's grave and left a letter with Tombkeeper Aldous:
  her uncle opened the gate under Firetown, and she meant to close it; if
  she didn't come back, the stone is with her "somewhere in the fire".
- **The Firetown Expedition** (firetown.mjs): Elowen left a second page
  with Tombkeeper Aldous: a gate is shut with warden stone, Brood Stone and
  the old words. Archivist Zyrith (who refused Elowen twelve years ago and
  is sorry) gives a fragment of Velkarath's broken warden stone. Old Bram,
  a boatman at Westsea Harbour, rows across past the king's closed road;
  his grandson Tam rows back. Elowen is alive, grey and thin, a prisoner of
  the Fallen in Firetown's cells: they keep her because the rift likes a
  king's blood near it. The Ashen Gaoler, captain of the Fallen, wore the
  shard. At the rift, at the passage's end, the shard and the warden stone
  and the words "Under the world, be still" close it. Elowen gives
  **Dawnbreaker**, King Zilen's sword, and a letter to Old Wenna: "Do not
  keep the chair warm for me; keep the people warm." She stays in Firetown
  until she can walk the passage. (Per player: the rift is closed in each
  player's own story.)
  **Open threads:** Kraghul and the Horde still in the dunes above
  Eastreach (decision L); Morvane, still risen (decision K).
- **Homecoming** (homecoming.mjs): Elowen walks the passage at last, and
  Old Bram keeps his promise: he rows her to Westsea, and she sails to
  Kingshaven on the Royal Swan. She lives with Old Wenna, off the plaza,
  and refuses the chair: Lord Steward Halvard keeps governing, and she
  keeps him honest (decision R). Then dead men in King Zilen's colours
  walk at the old graves above the beach south-east of the city (the
  crossing's dead, the **risen king's-men**), led by the **Grey Herald**,
  who carries her uncle's letter: "Niece. The chair is mine by blood.
  Give it up, or I will come and take it. M." She signs "E." because she
  learned it from him. She gives the **gaol-chain belt**, the chain from
  her cell, broken; the achievement is *The Chair Stays Cold*. (Per
  player: she is in her cell, or at Wenna's, by each player's own story.)
  **Open threads:** when Morvane "comes himself"; the crown, still on
  King Zilen's brow in the City of Light.
- New human characters are refugees still in the old world (decision I):
  the tutorial ("From Refugee to Soldier...") is at Westsea Harbour, where
  they wait for a berth east, enlist and are trained; the teleport stone
  puts them aboard one of the last ships, which lands at Kingshaven. It is
  the last of the crossing.

## The peoples of the new lands

- **Elves (Aelindor)** were here long before. Their Elder Council argued a
  week, then gave the refugees the western coast and seed for one spring.
  The **grey blight** in their groves began the year the ships came; it
  smells of ash and old iron. The Green Wardens fight it. The elves suspect
  the wind carries it from the burning across the sea (it is really a
  second gate wearing thin: decision P, not in game).
- **Drow (Velkarath)** are elves who went below in the first age. The
  surface songs call it **the Sundering** and say they went in pride; the
  Drow say they went on guard, to keep the gates. They felt the Firetown
  gate open: the deep shook for three days, lava rose in the east
  galleries, and the **ember dragon** woke.
- **Lizardfolk (Ssarakh)** are the oldest people; the brood remembers
  everything, egg to egg. Their elders cut the warden stones. The **Brood
  Stone** in Ssarakh's plaza is the last one whole (a shard of it, carried
  by Elowen, closed the Firetown rift). The fallen of the brood wake
  beside it.
- **The Dry Ones** (the Sand Kings of the Eastreach desert) are lizardfolk
  who left the mire long ago. They bow to Kraghul, who raids the dunes
  above Eastreach: the Horde's foothold on this side of the sea.
- **The wyrms** (ember, stone and frost dragons) wake when the world is
  torn. They all woke when the gate opened. They are old to humans but young
  to dragonkind: elder dragons exist elsewhere (Ithrae: "Old, you call them?
  To you. The brood remembers their mothers.").
- **The duergar** (grey dwarves) live deep below and dig upward. In
  Velkarath's caverns they raid the Drow (*The Deep Patrol*: they killed
  Jhaelra's lost patrol). Under Greystone Mine they are cutting toward the
  mithril of the deep seam (*The Old Drift*, below). They dug toward it
  once before, twenty years ago, then went quiet; why they came back is
  open.

## Greystone Mine (greystone.mjs, *The Old Drift*)

- The refugees' mine in the hills north of Kingshaven. **Overseer Garrick**
  runs it; **Old Bryn** has swung a pick there for forty years.
- **The Old Drift** caved in twenty years ago with six miners inside, among
  them Bryn's brother **Wil Ashby**. It was never dug out. Scratched in the
  rock at its end: "SIX OF US. AIR GOING. THEY ARE DIGGING. NOT OURS. W.A."
  The duergar's digging brought the roof down (decision J). Then they went
  quiet for twenty years.
- About a year ago the knocking began again: Bryn hears it below the deep
  seam. It is the great iron drum of **Thrumgar the Grey Warlord**,
  beating time for the duergar digging up from the **Grey Delve**, a hall
  cut square under the drift. Tunnel crawlers driven up into the old sump
  were the first sign.
- In the quests: crawlers in the sump; the drift opened, the delvers at its
  end, and Wil's brass lamp brought home to Bryn; then Thrumgar killed and
  the drum silenced ("First quiet in a year. Wil can rest.").
- Once a player has silenced the drum (gs_knocking_done), the mine says so:
  Bryn ("It's quiet now"), Cook Dagna and Overseer Garrick.
- **Open threads:** where the duergar come from, and whether the Grey
  Delve's tunnels run down to the caverns round Velkarath; why they came
  back, twenty years after the drift fell.

## The southern gate (greybeneath.mjs, *The Grey Beneath*)

- The grey blight came back to the withered grove after the Green Wardens
  healed it: it rises from a gate wearing thin under the grove (decision
  P), whose heart is warm to the touch.
- Kraghul's Dry Ones (**dry one diggers**) are widening the **root-crack**
  among the grey roots at the grove's heart, which breathes heat and the
  smell of ash and old iron; they carry **ashen brands**, Kraghul's horned
  head burned into iron. One of his horned Horde, the **Ash-Sworn
  Breaker**, was set to break the gate.
- The heartstones of the waking shrine are warden stone, cut by the
  lizardfolk elders (Ithrae; decision Q). Three of them, set round the
  crack, **pin** the gate: it holds, cold, but it is **not shut**.
- Drow learn more from Priestess Ilvra (*A Strand Pulled Thin*): the
  Spider Queen's web binds the gates, and its southern strand is pulled
  thin (decision O).
- Faelwen gives the **Thornward Cloak** (Green Wardens' work, a thread
  from the healed grove) for holding the door; the achievement is *The
  Door Held*.

## Canon decisions (2026-10-15)

Decided by flan; live NPC dialogue follows them.

- **A. King Aldric is gone.** He is not in Kingshaven and not in the old
  world: dead or missing, and no one knows which. Lord Steward Halvard
  governs Kingshaven in Elowen's name, beside the empty chair.
- **B. The wyrms are young.** The ember, stone and frost wyrms are old to
  humans but young to dragonkind. Elder dragons exist elsewhere (future
  endgame content).
- **C. The crown was bought. (SECRET: players only ever get a hint.)** The
  robber boss in *The King's Crown* was paid to fetch King Zilen's crown by
  an agent of Morvane. In the game Aldous only says "Thieves melt gold; they
  don't keep crowns. Someone paid for this one." Never name Morvane (or his
  agent) in the game.
- **D. The old world is dying by emptying, not burning.** Most people sailed
  east; the ones left are stubborn, poor or old. Its towns should read as
  thinned out and hanging on, not busy or thriving.
- **E. Renames to end name clashes.** Herbalist Wenna is now Herbalist
  Linnet; Reeve Tamsin is now Reeve Osric (a man); Tamsin the Tailor is now
  Corra the Tailor. Old Wenna and Old Tamsin keep their names. Quest ids
  and flags did not change.

**Open threads these create:**
- What happened to King Aldric (dead, missing, taken?). Left open on
  purpose (decision M).
- Why Morvane wanted his father's crown. (Answered by decision K.)
- The elder dragons: where they are, and what they make of the young wyrms.

## Canon decisions (2026-10-21)

Decided by flan (canon cleanup, part 2).

- **F. The crown still stands.** Kingshaven is under the crown of Zilen in
  law: Aldric is missing, not deposed, and Elowen is his heir. The King's
  Watch, the Royal Guard, the Royal Teller and "the crown" in everyday talk
  all stay.
- **G. Old-world content is kept as relics of better days.** The original
  game's signs keep their words but read as old and faded; its living NPCs
  keep their character but sound like people hanging on in an emptied
  land.
- **H. No out-of-world text anywhere.** Player graffiti, builders' notes and
  swearing are removed from every sign and NPC.
- **I. The human tutorial happens in the old world, before sailing.** The
  player is a refugee waiting for a berth east at Westsea, enlists, is
  trained, and the teleport stone puts them aboard one of the last ships,
  which lands at Kingshaven ("the last of the crossing").
- **J. The duergar dug toward Greystone once before.** Twenty years ago
  their digging brought the Old Drift's roof down, then they went quiet.
  About a year ago the drum began again. The open thread is why they came
  back.

## Canon decisions (2026-10-21, pillars)

Decided by flan. Most were lore only when decided; each says how far it is built.

- **K. Morvane wants the crown and the chair.** (PARTLY IN GAME: the Grey
  Herald's letter (Homecoming) shows he wants the chair. The crown's
  power over the dead is still secret.) The crown of Zilen commands the kingdom's dead: that is
  why the dead marched in the old king's colours (Old Wenna's father).
  Morvane never needed the fire; he needs to be crowned. His agent bought
  King Zilen's crown (decision C), and Aldous's "Whoever paid for it will
  want it again" stands. Kingshaven's empty throne is his prize: the dead
  are his, and the chair would give him the living.
- **L. The pact between Morvane and Kraghul is broken.** With the Firetown
  rift shut, Kraghul and his Horde are stranded on this side of the sea
  and want a gate opened; Morvane wants the gates kept shut, because the
  Horde is now his rival. Kraghul's story is the gates; Morvane's is the
  crown.
- **M. King Aldric's fate is deliberately undecided.** Build nothing that
  answers it.
- **N. Elowen comes home to Kingshaven and refuses the crown.** She would
  rather the chair stay empty than be a prize. Morvane hunts her anyway.
  (BUILT: Homecoming, homecoming.mjs.)
- **O. The Spider Queen is the Weaver of the seals.** (HINTED TO DROW ONLY:
  Priestess Ilvra's *A Strand Pulled Thin*, sg_web, speaks of her web.) A
  first-age power whose web binds the seven gates. The Drow went below to
  serve her vigil, and her mark on every drow's palm (the needle in the
  welcome rite) ties them to it: that is the "duty" the Drow speak of.
- **P. The grey blight is a second gate wearing thin.** (PARTLY IN GAME:
  *The Grey Beneath*. Ithrae names the heartstones warden stone in
  *Cut by No Elf*, and once that quest is done Faelith says she was wrong
  about the wind.) A gate under the southern woods, near Aelindor, is thinning;
  the blight is what seeps through, which is why it smells of ash and old
  iron and comes back however often the Green Wardens cut it. This is the
  gate Kraghul goes after (decision L). The Spider Queen's priests know of
  it. It lies under the withered grove, far east of Aelindor (The Grey
  Beneath).

- **Q. The shrine on the mountains west of Aelindor was a warden post.**
  Its gargoyles are the keepers, which wake when the door below grows
  thin, and heartstones are slivers of warden stone, cut by the lizardfolk
  elders. A sliver can pin a gate but never shut it; only whole warden
  stone shuts one.

**Open threads these create:**
- What Morvane will try next for the crown.
- Which gate Kraghul goes after: the southern one (decision P), under
  the withered grove. How near it is to breaking, now it is pinned.
- The Spider Queen (decision O): a hook for the Drow Priesthood's quests.
- A true seal for the southern gate needs whole warden stone, and the only
  whole one left is the Brood Stone in Ssarakh ("and it stays", Ithrae).
- Kraghul knows someone stands in his way: his Breaker is dead and his
  door pinned.
- What Elowen does in Kingshaven if she won't rule. (Answered by
  decision R.)
- When Morvane "comes himself" (his letter's threat).
- The crown, still on King Zilen's brow in the City of Light.

## The map at a glance

Two lands with a sea between them.

**The old world (Zilen), in the west.**

- **The City of Light** still holds. King Zilen's graveyard is at its
  north-west corner.
- **Midnight**, with the Izaru mine under it.
- **Riverside** and **Southfield**.
- **Westsea Harbour**, where the ships left from.
- **The east field.** Beyond its tree line is the **Frozen Reach**.
- **The toxic swamp** in the west.
- **A walled enclave in the far south.** The Champions' Gauntlet and the
  Fire Orb vault are there.
- **Firetown**, on its island in the southern sea.

The way to Midnight and the Izaru mine is kept a mystery on purpose: only
Old Sailor Wick's tale hints at it.

**The new lands, in the east.**

- **Kingshaven**, on the west coast. **Greystone Mine** is in the hills to
  its north. The King's Watch post, the barrow valley and the Duelling Ring
  are to its east.
- **Aelindor**, the elven city, in the south.
  - The Moonwell is at its heart, and Aelindor Landing is the pier to its
    north.
  - The shrine of the waking stones is on the mountains to its west.
  - The withered grove is far to its east, and The Circle's wood lies
    between them.
- **Velkarath**, below the ground. Three cave stairs come up on surface
  mountains, and only drow may take them.
- **Ssarakh**, the lizardfolk's Mire City, on the swamp isles.
- **Eastreach**, a ferry port on the eastern continent. The Dry Ones' dunes
  are to its north, where Kraghul raids.
- **The southern desert**, wide and mostly empty, with **Last Water** at
  its spring.

**Ferries.** Ships run between Westsea, Aelindor Landing, Eastreach,
Kingshaven and Ssarakh.

## The southern desert and Last Water (BUILT 2026-10-29: rumours only)

- **Last Water** is a walled outpost round a spring in the southern
  desert: the last water before the deep sand. Caravans fill their skins
  there.
  - **Wellkeeper Amsel** keeps the spring clean, and nobody owns it ("Water's
    free. Trouble isn't.").
  - **Ssuli the Trader** is a lizardfolk who left the crowded mire for the
    quiet of the sand.
  - A guard keeps the gate, and travellers sleep in a rest tent.
- The desert itself:
  - Coyotes come near the walls at dusk.
  - Further out are sand worms.
  - "Further than that, nobody comes back to say."
- Drow come in out of the west now and then, paying in silver, and don't
  say where from. (One of Velkarath's cave stairs opens at the desert's
  western edge. No one at Last Water knows that.)
- **Open, on purpose:** what lies in the deep sand. Nothing is decided.
  Any story here starts as a proposal, and the outpost should keep only
  rumours until flan picks one.
